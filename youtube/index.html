About

A personal-use app. It uploads the Learning English with Sophia channel's videos to its owner's own YouTube channel, always as private, with the already approved title, description, thumbnail and playlists. The owner reviews and publishes them in YouTube Studio. It is not offered to the public. See the privacy policy.
